    Footer Section¶

    The Footer section appears at the bottom of every page on your store. It's a crucial place for navigation, legal links, newsletter signups, and social media connections. You can customize it with multiple columns containing different types of content.

    Use the footer to: - Provide quick navigation to important pages - Display social media links - Collect newsletter subscribers - Share store information (hours, location, contact details) - Display legal links (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, etc.)

    Adding and Managing This Section¶

    The footer is a permanent section that appears on all pages. You can customize it through the Theme Editor by clicking on the footer section at the bottom of any page.

    To learn more about working with sections and blocks in Shopify themes, please refer to Shopify's official guide on Using sections and blocks.

    Section Settings¶

    Layout¶

    • Color scheme — Choose the color scheme for the footer background and text. Default: Scheme 2.
    • Section type — Choose the footer width:
    • Edge to Edge — Footer spans the full width of the screen
    • Box — Footer stays within a container with side margins

    Default: Box. * Show policy links — Enable to automatically display links to your store policies (Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, Refund Policy, etc.). These are managed in your Shopify admin under Settings > Policies. Default: Disabled.

    Blocks¶

    The footer is built with blocks that create columns of content. You can add up to 8 navigation or content blocks, plus one social media and newsletter block.

    Socials & Newsletter Block¶

    This special block combines social media links and a newsletter signup form. You can only add one of these blocks.

    Social Media Settings: * Color scheme — Choose the color scheme specifically for this block. Default: Scheme 1. * Heading — The heading above your social media icons. Default: "Social media". * Heading font-weight — Choose Normal or Bold. Default: Bold. * Content — Add descriptive text encouraging visitors to follow you. Default: "Follow us on social media". Supports rich text.

    Newsletter Settings: * Heading — The heading above the newsletter signup form. Default: "Newsletter". * Heading font-weight — Choose Normal or Bold. Default: Bold. * Content — Explain the benefits of subscribing. Default includes privacy policy mention. Supports rich text.

    Note: The actual social media links are managed in your theme settings under Social Media. The icons displayed here reflect those settings.

    Navigation Block¶

    Add navigation menus to create footer columns with links. You can add up to 8 of these blocks.

    Settings: * Heading — The title for this navigation column. Default: "Navigation". * Navigation menu — Select which menu to display in this column. Default: Footer menu. You create and manage these menus in your Shopify admin under Navigation.

    Content Block¶

    Add custom text content to create informational footer columns. You can add up to 8 of these blocks.

    Settings: * Heading — The title for this content column. Default: "Our shop". * Content — Add any information you want to share (store hours, address, contact info, etc.). Default: "Open every weekday from 09:00 to 18:00". Supports rich text.

    Default Footer Layout¶

    When you first add the footer section, it includes: - 1 Social & Newsletter block - 4 Navigation blocks

    You can customize this by adding, removing, or rearranging blocks to suit your needs.

    Usage Tips¶

    • Footer organization: A common footer structure uses 4-5 columns:
    • Column 1: About/company info
    • Column 2: Customer service links
    • Column 3: Product categories
    • Column 4: Legal/policies

    • Column 5: Social & newsletter

    • Navigation menus: Create clear, focused menus for your footer in Shopify admin (Online Store > Navigation). Keep each menu to 5-8 links for readability.

    • Newsletter privacy: Always mention your privacy policy when collecting email addresses. This builds trust and may be required by law in some regions.

    • Mobile layout: Footer columns automatically stack vertically on mobile devices for easy reading.

    • Policy links: Enable "Show policy links" to automatically add your store policies. Make sure you've set these up in Shopify admin under Settings > Policies first.

    • Social media: Update your social media links in Theme Settings > Social Media. Any links you leave empty won't show icons in the footer.
