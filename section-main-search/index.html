    Search Results Page Template¶

    The Search Results page displays products, articles, and pages that match a customer's search query. This template controls how search results are organized and displayed, making it easy for customers to find what they're looking for.

    Important Note¶

    This template only appears on search results pages (/search). You customize it by: 1. Going to your Theme Editor 2. In the page selector, choose Search or navigate to /search?q=test in your browser 3. Adjusting the Search section settings

    Template Settings¶

    Layout¶

    • No margin bottom — Enable to remove bottom spacing below this section. Default: Enabled.

    • Color scheme — Choose the background color for the search info card. Default: Scheme 2.

    Heading¶

    • Heading tag — HTML tag for the search term heading (H1-H6 or Span). Default: H1.

    • Heading size — Visual size of the search term:

    • Title 1 — Largest
    • Title 2 — Large
    • H1-H6 — Standard sizes

    Default: H1.

    • Heading font weight — Text thickness. Range: 100-900. Default: 700 (bold).

    Display Settings¶

    • Results per page — How many results to show before pagination. Range: 1-50. Default: 24.

    • Products per row (desktop) — Number of product columns on desktop. Options: col-md-3 (4 columns), col-md-4 (3 columns), col-md-6 (2 columns). Default: col-md-4 (3 columns).

    What the Search Page Shows¶

    The search results page displays three types of content that match the search query:

    Products¶

    • Displayed as product cards with images, titles, and prices
    • Use your theme's standard product card styling
    • Sortable using the dropdown menu

    Articles (Blog Posts)¶

    • Shown as article cards with featured images and titles
    • Link directly to the full blog post
    • Help drive traffic to your content

    Pages¶

    • Displayed as simple cards with page titles
    • Link to the full page
    • Useful for finding policy pages, about pages, etc.

    All results appear in a mixed grid, with products typically displayed most prominently.

    Search Features¶

    Sort Options¶

    Customers can sort search results by: - Relevance (default—best matches first) - Price (low to high or high to low) - Newest first - Best selling - Alphabetically (A-Z or Z-A)

    The sort dropdown appears in the top-right of the search info card.

    Results Count¶

    The page automatically displays how many total results were found for the search term.

    Pagination¶

    If there are more results than fit on one page, pagination controls appear at the bottom, allowing customers to browse through all results.

    How Search Works¶

    Shopify's built-in search indexes: - Product titles - Product descriptions - Product variants and SKUs - Product tags - Product types - Product vendors - Collection titles - Page titles and content - Blog post titles and content

    Search is: - Case-insensitive — "camera" and "Camera" return the same results - Partial matching — Searching "run" finds "running", "runner", etc. - Multi-word — Searching "blue shirt" finds products with both words

    Usage Tips¶

    • Empty state: If no results are found, Shopify displays a "No results" message. Consider adding suggested collections or products to this page.

    • Search bar placement: Make sure your search bar is easily accessible in your header (configured in Theme Settings > Header).

    • Product titles matter: Well-written product titles with relevant keywords improve search results.

    • Tag strategy: Use consistent, descriptive tags—they're searchable and help customers find products.

    • Page content: Optimize your page content (About, FAQ, etc.) with keywords customers might search for.

    • Blog integration: Having a blog increases searchable content on your site, improving the usefulness of search.

    Improving Search Results¶

    Optimize Product Data¶

    Make products easier to find by: - Writing clear, descriptive product titles - Including relevant keywords in descriptions - Adding comprehensive tags - Filling in product type and vendor fields - Including variant names customers might search for

    Synonym Groups¶

    In Shopify, you can create synonym groups to handle common variations: - "tshirt" = "t-shirt" = "t shirt" - "sneaker" = "trainer" = "athletic shoe"

    Set these up in Settings > Store > Search in your Shopify admin.

    Stop Words¶

    Shopify automatically ignores common words like "the", "and", "or" to improve search relevance.

    Best Practices¶

    • Results per page: 24 results balances discoverability with page load time. Adjust based on your catalog size.

    • Mixed content types: Showing products, articles, and pages in the same results helps customers find whatever they're looking for.

    • Clear headings: The search term is displayed as the heading (e.g., "red dress") so customers know what they searched for.

    • Sort accessibility: Make sure the sort dropdown is visible and easy to use, especially on mobile devices.

    • Mobile layout: Products automatically stack into fewer columns on mobile (typically 2 columns). Test to ensure cards remain readable.

    • No results handling: If customers frequently search for items you don't have, consider:

    • Adding those products to your catalog
    • Creating content around those topics
    • Redirecting common "no result" searches to relevant collections

    Search Analytics¶

    Monitor search behavior in your Shopify analytics:

    1. Go to Analytics > Reports in your Shopify admin
    2. Look for Online store search reports
    3. See:
    4. Top search terms
    5. Searches with no results
    6. Search-to-purchase conversion

    Use this data to: - Identify popular products to promote - Find gaps in your product catalog - Improve product titles and descriptions - Create content around popular searches

    Enhancing Search¶

    Search Suggestions¶

    Enable search suggestions in your header (Theme Settings > Header) to show popular searches or collections as customers type.

    Filters on Search Results¶

    While basic search shows all matching results, customers can use your collection filters after clicking through to specific categories.

    Third-Party Search Apps¶

    For advanced search features, consider Shopify apps that offer: - Autocomplete with thumbnails - Typo tolerance - Advanced filtering - Search analytics - Merchandising controls

    Common Search Issues¶

    Products not appearing in search: - Ensure products are published and available - Check product titles and descriptions contain relevant keywords - Verify products aren't hidden or password-protected

    Irrelevant results: - Remove unnecessary tags that create false matches - Refine product descriptions to focus on relevant terms - Use more specific product titles

    Too many results: - Encourage customers to use more specific search terms - Implement synonym groups to consolidate variants - Ensure product categorization is clear

    Technical Notes¶

    • Search results are generated by Shopify's search engine—you can't customize the search algorithm itself.

    • The search parameter in URLs is ?q=search-term.

    • Search is available in all languages if you have a multi-language store.

    • Search respects your inventory settings—out-of-stock products appear based on your visibility preferences.
