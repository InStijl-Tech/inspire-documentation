    Article Page Template¶

    The Article Page template displays individual blog posts with full content, featured images, author information, and social sharing options. This is where customers read your blog articles in detail.

    Template Settings¶

    Colors & Layout¶

    • Color scheme — Background color and text style. Default: Scheme 1.

    Article Image¶

    • Image width — Control featured image width:
    • Container — Image stays within page width
    • Box — Image stretches full width (edge-to-edge)

    Default: Box.

    • Image height — Featured image height in pixels. Range: 300-800px. Default: 500px.

    Heading¶

    • Heading tag — HTML tag for article title (H1-H6 or Span). Default: H1.
    • Heading size — Visual size of the title (Title 1, Title 2, or H1-H6). Default: H1.
    • Heading font weight — Text thickness (100-900). Default: 700.

    Content Width¶

    • Container width — Maximum width for article text:
    • Default — Uses theme's standard container width
    • Custom — Set a specific width (Range: 600-1200px)

    Default: Default.

    Tip: Narrower content (around 700-800px) improves readability for long-form articles.

    Article Info¶

    • Show author — Display author name and publication date below title. Default: Enabled.

    Reading Experience¶

    • Enable progress bar — Show reading progress indicator at top of page. Default: Disabled.
    • Progress bar color — Color for the progress bar. Default: Your primary color.
    • Enable back to top button — Show button to scroll back to top. Default: Enabled.

    Social Sharing¶

    • Share buttons — Enable social sharing buttons. Default: Enabled.
    • Share title — Text above share buttons. Default: "Share this article".

    Platforms included: - Facebook - Twitter (X) - Pinterest - Email - WhatsApp

    Article Structure¶

    The page displays: 1. Featured Image — Large hero image at top (if article has one) 2. Article Title — Main headline 3. Article Info — Author and date (if enabled) 4. Article Content — Full blog post body with formatting 5. Share Buttons — Social sharing options (if enabled) 6. Back to Top Button — Floating button (if enabled)

    Managing Articles¶

    Article content is managed in your Shopify admin:

    1. Go to Online Store > Blog Posts
    2. Select your blog
    3. Click on an article to edit:
    4. Title — Article headline
    5. Content — Full article text (rich text editor)
    6. Excerpt — Short summary for article cards
    7. Featured Image — Main image (recommended: 1200x800px)
    8. Author — Writer name
    9. Tags — Topics for organization
    10. SEO — Meta description and URL
    11. Save changes

    Usage Tips¶

    • Featured images: Always include high-quality featured images. They're displayed prominently and shared on social media.

    • Formatting: Use the rich text editor to:

    • Add headings for structure
    • Break text into short paragraphs
    • Include bullet points and lists
    • Insert images throughout longer articles

    • Add links to related products or articles

    • Content width: If you have long-form articles, consider using a narrower container width (700-800px) for easier reading.

    • Progress bar: Enable for longer articles (1000+ words) so readers can see how far they've read.

    • Author info: Display author names to build credibility and personal connection.

    • Share buttons: Make it easy for readers to share your content. Social sharing drives traffic.

    • Back to top: Helpful for long articles so readers can easily navigate back to the top.

    • Mobile readability: Test articles on mobile. Ensure images scale properly and text is readable.

    Best Practices¶

    Content¶

    • Compelling titles: Write clear, benefit-driven headlines that make people want to read.
    • Strong opening: Hook readers in the first paragraph.
    • Scannable format: Use headings, short paragraphs, bullet points, and images.
    • Value-focused: Provide useful information, not just promotional content.
    • Call-to-action: End with a clear next step (shop products, read related articles, leave a comment).

    Images¶

    • Featured image: Use high-quality, relevant images (recommended: 1200x800px or 16:9 ratio).
    • In-content images: Break up text with relevant images every 300-500 words.
    • Alt text: Add descriptive alt text to all images for accessibility and SEO.
    • Consistent style: Use similar image styles across all articles for visual consistency.

    SEO¶

    • Meta description: Write compelling descriptions (150-160 characters) in the article editor.
    • URL handle: Use clear, keyword-rich URLs (e.g., /blogs/news/summer-collection-guide).
    • Internal links: Link to related products, collections, or other articles.
    • Keywords: Include relevant keywords naturally in title, headings, and content.
    • Tags: Use consistent tags for topic organization and SEO.

    Engagement¶

    • Enable comments: Allow customer interaction (if your blog supports comments).
    • Respond promptly: Reply to comments to build community.
    • Social promotion: Share articles on your social media channels.
    • Email newsletter: Feature new articles in email campaigns.

    Reading Progress Bar¶

    The progress bar feature shows a thin colored line at the top of the page that fills as customers scroll through the article. It helps readers: - See how much content remains - Gauge time commitment - Feel progress toward completion

    When to use: - Long-form content (1000+ words) - In-depth guides and tutorials - Story-driven articles

    When to skip: - Short articles (under 500 words) - News briefs or announcements - Image-heavy posts

    Social Sharing Strategy¶

    Social sharing buttons help amplify your content reach:

    • Placement: Sharing buttons appear at the end of articles after readers finish.
    • Multiple platforms: Offer several options to match reader preferences.
    • Pre-filled content: Sharing includes article title and URL automatically.
    • Featured image: When shared, your featured image appears on social platforms.

    Optimize for sharing: - Use eye-catching featured images (they appear in social previews) - Write compelling titles (they become the share text) - Add meta descriptions (appear in link previews) - Test how shares look on different platforms
